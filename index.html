What we build

Five parts, one system

Frontend

The cockpit

One screen that shows what needs you today.

  • Made for the phone in your hand, and it installs like an app
  • Any language, including right-to-left scripts
  • No manual. If a new hire cannot use it in ten minutes, we rebuild it
Backend

Rules that hold even when people forget

The business rules live in the database, not just on the screen.

  • Production cannot start until the client approves the text, even if someone tries another way in
  • Totals are calculated in one place, so every screen and report agrees
  • One database per client in the client’s own account, built from one codebase, so a fix reaches every client
AI layer

Agents that work like good staff

Three lanes, and the line between them is simple.

  • Answers on its own: opening hours, what you offer, how delivery works. Facts it reads from your data
  • Drafts for a person: quotes, order summaries, booking requests, replies to unhappy customers
  • Never on its own: discounts, refunds, payments, confirming a date, anything that commits the business
  • Cloud models, or local open models when data cannot leave the building. Every action logged, spend capped, a kill switch within reach
Security

Built to survive a security review

  • Strong sign-in, two-step for admins, sessions that expire
  • Owner, staff and view-only roles enforced in the database, with row-level rules tested by trying to break them
  • An append-only record of who did what, including what the AI suggested and what a person approved
  • Encryption in transit and at rest, secrets kept in a vault, never in code
  • Prompt-injection tests, limited tool access, and an independent penetration test for sensitive systems
Continuity

You can run it without us

  • Hosting, database, messaging and domain accounts are yours. We are invited admins, nothing more
  • The code is delivered in your own repository, with a plain-language runbook
  • Backups you can restore yourself, tested together before handover
  • Keeps working through power and internet cuts, then syncs when they return
  • An on-premise option with local AI models for sensitive data

In practice

What changes in a business like yours

Hotels and hospitality

Calls and messages go unanswered at night. The agent replies in the guest’s language, checks availability and queues a booking request. Staff confirm in the morning.

Distributors

Orders arrive as texts and voice notes. The agent turns each one into a structured order. Nothing waits on someone’s memory.

Manufacturers

Paper job cards and handwritten logs become one live view of production, stock and deadlines, with alerts before something stops the line.

Clinics

Appointment requests and reminders by message, so fewer people forget to come. Any medical question goes straight to a person.

Retail and stores

Daily sales, stock and till data checked automatically. Odd numbers raise a flag before month end, not after.
